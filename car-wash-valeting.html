Frequently asked questions

Car wash and valeting FAQs.

How much is a hand car wash in Farnham?

Blue Service starts from £13.00 for a small car. Red Service starts from £28.00 and Gold Service starts from £35.00. Prices vary by vehicle size and type, and all listed prices include VAT.

What is included in the Gold Service?

Gold Service includes hand shampoo, rinse wax, hand drying, wheel and tyre shine, interior vacuuming, boot cleaning, dashboard polishing, windows, and a quick wipe of plastics and leather seats.

Do you offer full valeting?

Yes. Full Valet interior and exterior services are available, with prices on request. Use the Full Valet WhatsApp button above to ask the team.

Do larger vehicles cost more?

Prices vary depending on vehicle size and type, including larger cars, SUVs, people carriers and vans. Check the relevant service price list above for your vehicle.
